How to Read It

AdjO / AdjD — points scored / allowed per 100 possessions, adjusted for opponent quality. D1 average AdjO is 105.2. Lower AdjD is better.
Barthag — Barttorvik's power rating: the chance a team beats an average D1 opponent. The single best one-number summary here.
Adj Tempo — adjusted possessions per 40 minutes. D1 average is 71.1.
eFG% — effective field-goal percentage; counts a made three as 1.5 baskets. The most important of the Four Factors.
TO% / TORD% — share of possessions ending in a turnover, committed vs. forced. A pressure defense against a loose-ballhandling team is a classic March edge.
ORB% / DRB% — offensive rebounds grabbed, and (as Barttorvik reports it) the offensive rebound rate allowed to opponents — lower DRB% is better.
FTR / FTRD — free-throw rate: free-throw attempts per field-goal attempt, earned vs. allowed. Matters most in close, late games.
3P Rate — share of shots taken from three. Volume is more stable than 3P% itself, so it tells you how a team's scoring is built.
WAB — Wins Above Bubble: resume credit for wins against the schedule actually played. Negative means the resume is underwater this early.
